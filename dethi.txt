Track C: Fault-Tolerant Replicated Key-Value Store
• Textbook & Syllabus Alignment: Lecture 02, 06, 07, 08 | Chapter 5 & Chapter
7 (Sec. 7.1–7.5).
• System Goal: Build a distributed key-value storage cluster that replicates data
across multiple storage nodes to provide high availability and configurable
consistency guarantees.
• Individual Module Ownership:
o Student 1 — Primary / Coordinator Node & Write Protocol:
Implements a primary-based consistency protocol (or quorum consensus).
Receives client PUT/DELETE updates, assigns monotonically increasing
version numbers, and propagates updates synchronously or
asynchronously to backup replicas.
o Student 2 — Backup Replica Nodes & Catch-Up Recovery: Manages
local persistent key-value storage on backup nodes, processes replication
streams from the primary, detects missed updates upon rejoining after a
crash, and synchronizes stale state.
o Student 3 — Smart Client Proxy & Client-Centric Consistency:
Implements a client-side middleware library (Fig. 3-10) that provides
Read-Your-Writes and Monotonic Reads guarantees. Caches recent
reads locally and automatically fails over to a backup replica if a server
times out.
